## 1. Verify rights and prepare the downloads

1. Audit existing rights records first. Check the authoring project documents, original licence or owner policy, and the exact embedded texture dependency manifests. The 22 saved manifests record attribution and omit licence fields. Rights may be documented elsewhere. The scene's `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt` explicitly withholds a pack-level CC0 claim. Separate the MIT scene code, authored asset terms, and the actual notices for approved textures and other dependencies. Record the source and applicable terms for each selected pin and any distributed ancestor. Apply prior explicit owner policy where it covers these revisions and forms of redistribution. Bring only unresolved display, runtime or editable redistribution terms to the owner.
2. Preserve actual authoring attribution from the manifests and lineage. The current repaired cathedral pin records GPT-6 Astra and Codex. The three repaired dormer variants and pew record GPT-6.1 Sol and Codex, with the original house versions credited separately. Most other town entries record Claude Opus 5.5 and Claude Code. The crucifix records Gemini 4 Argon (High) and Antigravity. These labels describe recorded authoring provenance.
3. Generate into a staging directory from exact saved revisions. For each approved pin record the saved GLB SHA-256 and bytes, editable ZIP SHA-256 and bytes, model metrics and dimensions, review status, thumbnail and alt text. Require the gallery viewer and runtime downloads to resolve the same hash as the scene when offering the same revision. Reuse the sealed scene model for the viewer where practical. Generate editorial thumbnails from the selected revision, with dedicated exterior/interior scene captures after the final lighting review. Keep view-only and downloadable rights explicit.
4. Use the revision ZIP, dependency closure and deterministic aggregate archive tooling in `site/scripts/stage-asset-delivery.mjs` and `site/scripts/asset-delivery.mjs`. Preserve source, saved manifests, preview and dependency data. Include only approved history, report unavailable or withheld ancestors, and test import into a clean Kiln workspace. The existing delivery generator currently inserts CC0 into generated readmes and the runtime pack licence. Add an explicit approved notice contract for Cathedral Square before enabling its group. Include corresponding notices in each artifact, covering both aggregate profiles and asset downloads. Confirm missing terms fail the delivery gate.
5. Add `site/src/data/packs/cathedral-square.json` as the gallery projection of the scene's pin manifest plus approved editorial data. Generate the Cathedral Square group in `site/src/data/asset-delivery.json` and add exact mirror/media records through the existing `site/scripts/fetch-mirror.mjs` intake. Keep local preview paths independent of CDN availability. Build a portable scene-input ZIP of the sealed pack and notices so a fresh site checkout can reproduce the page without the sibling authoring workspaces. Bind the ZIP and its embedded pack to the same release and hashes.
