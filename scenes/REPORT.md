**M4 findings on three 0.186.0** (no dependency or pin change; [hitch notes](evidence/m4/hitches/README.md)): an instanced mesh with at most 1,024 instances keeps its matrices in a uniform buffer declared in its own vertex shader, so each compiles its own program or pipeline, lazily when it first comes into view (on WebGL2 a synchronous link of about 100 ms inside that frame), and uploads the whole buffer every frame it draws; a `DynamicDrawUsage` attribute is uploaded every frame whether or not it changed; and `compileAsync` builds node materials in slices across tasks, so many concurrent builds carry their temporaries into the old generation (about 250 MB with all 221 chains at once). The Farm avoids all three through documented API (R6-01, R6-03, R6-04); FARM-008 tells the other scenes.
